The "Columns" mode is now "Grid": drag accounts onto the edges of a cell, onto its center (swap) or along the border of the grid to arrange them in rows and columns, resize cells with the handles between them (or equalize them), and take an account out of the grid with its ✕ button. Cells never get smaller than a readable size, and the arrangement is kept between launches.
